# Phase II-F information boundary

Phase II-F planning uses the frozen Phase II-B `PredictionService`, the existing Phase II-E robust input contract, completed heat acknowledgements, and sensor rows whose 15-minute intervals have closed by the decision time. The maintenance eligibility gate reads typed 24-hour MILL_01 and PMP_01 probabilities and the latest four **posted** vibration rows; it rejects future publication times, unsafe fallbacks, missing predictions, insufficient sensor quality, and an asset in repair. It may require a second consecutive published risk observation. Classifier operating thresholds are not maintenance thresholds.

The optimizer receives only these public inputs and candidate service windows. It does not receive `events`, `latent_truth`, injected fault labels, wear, future failure times, true fault severity, or a future scenario tape. The closed-loop controller knows a simulation seed only to advance the executor. It passes `public_snapshot(...)` through the existing time-availability gate to the next prediction call. A heat already started, completed rolling feed, and completed service are immutable. Each retained replan record includes its decision time, available-information provenance, model version, public health evidence, candidate and selected service windows, and binding constraints.

The AI4I external benchmark is not used to set plant maintenance thresholds, infer lead time, or claim avoided downtime. The F1 mode publishes warnings without changing machine capacity or the E4 schedule; there is no validated mapping from a health probability to a capacity derating.

The synthetic executor alone applies the action to the wear/failure tape and physical availability. With no action it reproduces the frozen Phase II-E executor. A service stops its asset for 15 minutes, resets its wear to the configured post-service level, and draws a new life from that asset's named seeded wear stream. Pump service also excludes every induction heat during the service interval; mill service excludes rolling and mill power. Other exogenous streams are unchanged. This is a **synthetic replacement assumption**, not an established shop-floor procedure. All numeric intervention assumptions are declared in [maintenance_v1.yaml](../configs/maintenance_v1.yaml).

Hidden state is used only by the independent replay checker and the same-seed no-service counterfactual attribution. These evaluation tables are stored separately from prediction and optimizer inputs. A 24-hour risk probability does not identify the exact failure time. The planning heuristic assumes a uniform conditional failure time solely to rank feasible service windows; the replay, rather than that heuristic, determines physical outcomes. A service is called unnecessary within 24 hours only when the matched no-service run has no same-asset failure in that declared horizon. This does not establish that the service has no longer-term value. Failed runs and unavailable counterfactuals remain in the attempted-case denominator and attribution table.

The release postprocessor writes `maintenance_decision_outcomes.parquet` and `maintenance_recommendation_history.parquet` with explicit `evaluation_only` markers. These tables join decision-time public evidence to later replay status and counterfactual labels. They cannot be runtime predictors or current operator observations. Original public prediction, health and commitment histories remain separate and are not overwritten with future labels. Full physical replay outputs, matched comparisons, attribution and failure-diagnostic tables are evaluation artifacts, not feature tables.

The 201–210 seeds from Phase II-D remain frozen evaluation evidence and are absent from Phase II-F calibration, tuning and evaluation. Phase II-F development, validation and final seed groups are disjoint in [maintenance_split_v1.json](../configs/maintenance_split_v1.json). The exact upstream file hashes are protected by [phase2f_readonly_manifest.json](../configs/phase2f_readonly_manifest.json).

Final release audit: all 24 declared final cases retained; 253 full-suite tests passed after final evaluation; 3530 protected upstream hashes unchanged. Policy/design/model parameters were not modified. Reporting ledgers and final validation tables are EVALUATION-ONLY and cannot enter PredictionService. Failed full-day continuations are not independently accepted physical plans.
